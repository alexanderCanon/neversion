# Neversion

## 1. Overview

Neversion is a digital commerce brand focused on facilitating access to digital services. It operates as an intermediary platform that enables users to acquire digital products such as streaming services, mobile top-ups, gift cards, and other online services in a simple and accessible way.

The brand is primarily oriented toward users who face limitations in accessing digital platforms directly, whether due to payment barriers or preference for assisted purchasing.

---

## 2. Purpose

Neversion exists to promote and simplify access to digital services through a reliable and user-friendly approach.

The brand aims to reduce friction in digital commerce, especially for users who:
- Do not have access to credit or debit cards
- Prefer assistance when making online purchases
- Seek a simpler and more guided buying experience

---

## 3. Mission

To provide accessible, reliable, and transparent solutions for acquiring digital services, ensuring a smooth and secure experience for every customer.

---

## 4. Vision

To evolve into a scalable digital platform that integrates automation and intelligent systems, improving how users access and manage digital services.

Neversion seeks to position itself as a modern solution within the digital economy, leveraging technology to enhance accessibility and efficiency.

---

## 5. Value Proposition

Neversion offers a simplified way to access digital services by combining:

- Accessibility for users with limited payment options
- Personalized support throughout the purchasing process
- A focus on trust, transparency, and reliability

The brand bridges the gap between users and digital platforms, making digital consumption easier and more approachable.

---

## 6. Target Audience

Neversion is designed for:

- Individuals aged 17 to 35
- Users with low to moderate technological familiarity
- Customers located primarily in Guatemala
- People who:
  - Lack access to traditional online payment methods
  - Prefer delegating digital purchases to a trusted intermediary

---

## 7. Products and Services

Neversion provides access to a variety of digital services, including:

- Streaming platforms
- Mobile top-ups
- Gift cards
- Other digital services

These services are delivered through a streamlined and user-oriented process designed to minimize complexity.

---

The brand focuses on delivering:
- Reliable service

Its primary objective is to create a dependable environment where users feel confident when accessing digital services.

Neversion prioritizes a personalized and supportive experience.

Neversion is currently transitioning from a manual operation toward a more automated and scalable system.

## 12. About the Founder

Neversion was founded by Alexander Canon, a systems engineering student with a strong interest in technology and digital solutions.

With over 5 years of experience operating in the digital services space, the founder has developed the brand with a focus on adaptability, continuous learning, and practical problem-solving.

Originally launched under the name "New Experience," the brand evolved into "Neversion" to reflect a more distinct and scalable identity.
